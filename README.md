# Mining Royalty Settlement Reconciliation

Calculate mineral royalties from production, grade, recovery, sales value, and allowable deductions.

**Primary buyer:** Mineral owners, governments, and mining companies. **Evidence:** mineral leases, production, assays, recoveries, sales, benchmark prices, treatment charges, transportation, deductions, royalty rates, settlements, and payments.

Full local application built with React, Vite, Express, PostgreSQL, and OpenRouter. Includes 15 domain-specific capabilities, 105 custom AI workbench fields, three scenario-fill controls per feature, operational registers, workflow transitions, analytics, professional AI decision briefs, audit history, and at least 15 PostgreSQL records per capability.

## Domain capabilities

- Mineral lease library
- Mine production ingestion
- Assay grade validation
- Metallurgical recovery
- Payable metal calculation
- Benchmark price mapping
- Sales revenue reconciliation
- Treatment charge validation
- Refining charge validation
- Transportation deduction
- Royalty rate calculation
- Settlement statement audit
- Operator dispute workflow
- Cash reconciliation
- Mine commodity analytics

Run `./start.sh`, then open <http://127.0.0.1:4712>. API: `5712`.

Administrator: `runtime-admin@example.com` / `LocalDemo!2026`. Operator and reviewer credential buttons are available on the login page.
